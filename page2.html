Camera Settings for Sports Photography

Choosing the right camera settings can help photographers freeze action and capture sharp images during sporting events.

Recommended Camera Settings

There is no single camera setting that works for every sport. Lighting, movement, and the location of the event can change the settings a photographer needs.

These steps are a good starting point when preparing to photograph a fast-moving sport.

  1. Choose a fast shutter speed to freeze movement.
  2. Use a wide aperture to let more light into the camera.
  3. Adjust ISO depending on the available light.
  4. Use continuous autofocus to follow moving athletes.
